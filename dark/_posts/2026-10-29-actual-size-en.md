<div class="ds-tc-p" data-x="75" data-y="1110" data-w="390" data-see="drop" data-drop="1">
<p>Who poured water on me?</p>
<p>I was dried down to a speck. Nicely dried. Not eating, not drinking, not mad at anybody. Then a wet wad of cotton came jabbing down out of the sky and soaked me open. All eight legs, soaked out. Every one of them cramping.</p>
</div>
<div class="ds-tc-p" data-x="300" data-y="360" data-w="390" data-drop=".94">
<p>Where is this? The ground's fuzzy, grey-white, pitted all over. I know this. The sea chewed it. Took the sea two hundred years to chew it like this.</p>
<p>So where's the sea? There's one ditch, and in the ditch one puddle, twenty of me wide. The ditch used to have a lid on it. Long gone. That's why any old thing can poke in.</p>
</div>
<div class="ds-tc-p" data-x="805" data-y="150" data-w="390" data-drop=".88">
<p>The old ones in my family say: one day, it rained bowls. Big ones, little ones, lids, no lids, by the shipload, smacking into the mud. We moved into the smallest, founded a country, and stayed two hundred years.</p>
<p>Then one day the bowls went back up. With us in them. Did anybody ask us?</p>
</div>
<div class="ds-tc-p" data-x="805" data-y="820" data-w="390" data-drop=".82">
<p>Now there's a second sun up there, white, glaring, baking my lake smaller.</p>
<p>And behind the sun there's something else. An eye. Bigger than the whole country, wet, blinking.</p>
<p>You. Yes, you. What are you looking at?</p>
</div>
<div class="ds-tc-p" data-x="1420" data-y="960" data-w="390" data-drop=".6" data-flick="1">
<p class="ds-tc-ding">Ting!</p>
<p>What did you flick it for?! The whole country's humming, half my lake just slopped out. And you're muttering, it comes up through the ground like a quake: “Qianlong… export… pill box… sea-salvaged. The sea's eaten the glaze. Pity.”</p>
<p>Pity? That's our floor!</p>
</div>
<div class="ds-tc-p" data-x="760" data-y="1400" data-w="390" data-drop=".55" data-run="1">
<p>Let me tell you who I am. I've been frozen. I've been baked. I've been thrown up into the sky where there's no air, and I came back every time. You named us tardigrades. Slow steppers. Slow? Watch me run—</p>
<p>Can't. Lake's too small.</p>
</div>
<div class="ds-tc-p ds-tc-big" data-x="85" data-y="600" data-w="270" data-z="1.3" data-see="drop" data-drop=".48">
<p>Get your sun off me.</p>
<p>It's cooking my lake. I've been awake one minute! I haven't seen the whole country!</p>
<p>Off!</p>
<p>I've got needles! Two!</p>
</div>

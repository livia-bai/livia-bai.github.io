<p class="ds-sea-sum"><b>General synopsis</b>Low over the living room, moving slowly east, expected to fill near the curtains by dawn. A warm, humid airmass persists inside the duvet. Leaving it is not advised.</p>
<h3 class="ds-sea-h">Area forecasts</h3>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="pillow"><b>Pillow North</b>Westerly three or four, occasionally five with turning over. Good, becoming poor. Dreams, scattered. Exams, locally.</p>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="duvet"><b>Duvet Point</b>Exposed. Cold. One foot patrolling alone, now outside duvet jurisdiction, no sign of return.</p>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="glass"><b>Glass Reef</b>Half full. Calm. A moth came to sound the depth, then left without publishing its findings.</p>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="slippers"><b>Slipper Isles</b>Two islands, not adjacent. One facing east, one facing down.</p>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="desk"><b>Desk</b>One unread message at anchor. Wave height: about one heart. All vessels advised to deal with it in the morning.</p>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="rack"><b>Rack Strait</b>Still. A shirt holding the shape of someone who just stepped out of it.</p>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="fridge"><b>Fridge</b>Humming, force two. Stopping every forty minutes or so. For those few seconds, the whole area holds its breath.</p>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="door"><b>Door Gap</b>A thin line of light, seeping in from the hallway. Weak. Steady.</p>
<p class="ds-fc" data-sea="window"><b>Sill</b>Visibility: moon.</p>

<h3 class="ds-sea-h">Reports from coastal stations</h3>
<p class="ds-sea-coast">Kitchen tap: one drop every seven seconds, steady.<br>Street below: a car alarm, one chirp, then nothing, like someone saying half a sentence in their sleep.<br>Distant: a dog, three barks at something, then a change of mind.</p>
<h3 class="ds-sea-h">Navigational warning</h3>
<p class="ds-fc ds-sea-warn" data-sea="you">All lighthouses in the area are now extinguished except one, which is in your hand, at about thirty percent brightness.<br>When you have finished reading, please put it out too.</p>
<p class="ds-sea-end">That ends the forecast. The next will be issued when you wake.</p>

(function () {
  var root = document.querySelector('.ds-sea');
  if (!root) return;
  function light(name) {
    root.querySelectorAll('.is-lit').forEach(function (el) { el.classList.remove('is-lit'); });
    root.querySelectorAll('[data-sea="' + name + '"]').forEach(function (el) { el.classList.add('is-lit'); });
    var p = root.querySelector('.ds-fc[data-sea="' + name + '"]');
    if (p) p.scrollIntoView({ behavior: 'smooth', block: 'nearest' });
  }
  root.querySelectorAll('.ds-sea-a').forEach(function (g) {
    g.addEventListener('click', function () { light(g.getAttribute('data-sea')); });
    g.addEventListener('keydown', function (e) {
      if (e.key === 'Enter' || e.key === ' ') { e.preventDefault(); light(g.getAttribute('data-sea')); }
    });
  });
})();
</script>

title: "Then Am I a Tree?"

<div class="poem">My hair grows, turns yellow, and falls out.
Does that make me a tree?

If so,
my autumn must be very short —
one pass of a comb
and every leaf is down.

I have rings, too,
hidden in my birthday candles:
each one I blow out
adds another circle inside.

Waiting at the bus stop,
I'm a tree waiting for the wind.
When the wind comes, I get on and leave —
something a tree could never learn.

On rainy days I skip the umbrella on purpose
to find out what being watered feels like.
All I get is a sneeze —
trees don't do that either, I think.

But one afternoon
I sat on the balcony in the sun for a long time,
doing nothing, thinking nothing,
and felt myself
quietly grow a little taller.

So if someday
a bird decides to land on my shoulder,
to stay a while, to rest,
I'll stop asking.</div>

<p class="poem-end">🌳 Written in late October</p>

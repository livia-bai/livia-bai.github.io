Sounds familiar, doesn't it?

Hm? You're asking why it isn't *the Dark Side* of the moon?

Hehe, because I really love looking at the bright side on a clear night 🌕

## What's so good about the bright side?

The side of the moon that faces us is just really pretty:

- Sometimes it's a thin little curve, like a fingernail mark someone left by accident;
- Sometimes it's round and full, bright enough to throw your shadow on the ground;
- And those grey patches? Some people see a rabbit, some see a face. I see something a little different every time.

And it never hides away. Whatever city you're in, whatever night you look up, it shows you the very same face, as if to say: "I'm right here."

So this is the side where I put the things I make: projects I've built, new things I've learned, and all sorts of rambling notes.

## And the Dark Side?

Fun fact: the moon takes exactly as long to spin once as it does to circle the Earth, so it always keeps the same face turned towards us. The other side can't be seen from Earth at all.

But "can't be seen" doesn't mean "isn't there". The far side gets sunshine too. At new moon it's actually brighter than the side facing us. Whatever is going on over there, we just can't see it from Earth.

So you think there's no Dark Side?

...How would you know there isn't? 🌑
